<span style="font-size:30pt;color:#ffdd76ff;">To become more productive, stop hustling</span> 

<p style="text-align:center;margin:0">
</p>
<span style="font-size:11.142857551574707pt;color:#ffdd76ff;">Written by</span> <a href="https://alifeofproductivity.com/author/chris/" rel="noopener" class="external-link" target="_blank" style="font-size:11.142857551574707pt;">Chris Bailey</a> <span style="font-size:11.142857551574707pt;color:#ffdd76ff;">× October 20, 2020</span> 
<span style="font-size:11.142857551574707pt;color:#ffdd76ff;"><b>Takeaway:</b></span> <span style="font-size:11.142857551574707pt;color:#ffdd76ff;">The most productive people don’t hustle themselves into an early grave—they work with calm deliberateness, prioritizing thoughtfulness over speed. This will help you work with greater intention.</span>
<span style="font-size:11.142857551574707pt;color:#ffdd76ff;"><b>Estimated Reading Time:</b></span> <span style="font-size:11.142857551574707pt;color:#ffdd76ff;">3 minutes, 45s.</span>

<p style="text-align:center;margin:0">
</p>
<span style="font-size:11.142857551574707pt;color:#ffdd76ff;">There seem to be two schools of thought for how we should approach productivity advice.</span>  
<span style="font-size:11.142857551574707pt;color:#ffdd76ff;">The first is that we should</span> <span style="font-size:11.142857551574707pt;color:#ffdd76ff;"><b>hustle like crazy</b></span><span style="font-size:11.142857551574707pt;color:#ffdd76ff;">. After all, hustlers get $#!† done! They “rise and grind,” hustle all day, and head to bed exhausted—but they feel that way</span> <span style="font-size:11.142857551574707pt;color:#ffdd76ff;"><i>because</i></span> <span style="font-size:11.142857551574707pt;color:#ffdd76ff;">they were able to accomplish so much. Elon Musk—who advocates for working</span> <a href="https://www.cnbc.com/2018/11/05/elon-musk-on-working-120-hours-a-week-youll-go-bonkers.html" rel="noopener" class="external-link" target="_blank" style="font-size:11.142857551574707pt;">80 hour weeks</a> <span style="font-size:11.142857551574707pt;color:#ffdd76ff;">and has been prone to</span> <a href="https://www.nytimes.com/2018/08/16/business/elon-musk-interview-tesla.html" rel="noopener" class="external-link" target="_blank" style="font-size:11.142857551574707pt;">burn out</a><span style="font-size:11.142857551574707pt;color:#ffdd76ff;">—falls into this category. And it’s hard to argue with Elon’s success.</span>  
<span style="font-size:11.142857551574707pt;color:#ffdd76ff;">The second mindset favors working</span> <span style="font-size:11.142857551574707pt;color:#ffdd76ff;"><b>calmly and deliberately</b></span><span style="font-size:11.142857551574707pt;color:#ffdd76ff;">. This means planning more often before we get to work. When it does come time to complete a task, it happens with a deep intentionality. Over-working is rare. Warren Buffet—who</span> <a href="https://www.businessinsider.com/warren-buffett-daily-routine-2017-8?op=1#once-hes-in-the-office-he-hits-the-books-cnbc-reported-that-buffett-estimates-he-spends-80-of-his-day-reading-he-recommends-that-people-try-to-read-at-least-500-pages-a-day-11" rel="noopener" class="external-link" target="_blank" style="font-size:11.142857551574707pt;">spends</a> <span style="font-size:11.142857551574707pt;color:#ffdd76ff;">80% of each day reading, heads to work</span> <span style="font-size:11.142857551574707pt;color:#ffdd76ff;"><i>after</i></span> <span style="font-size:11.142857551574707pt;color:#ffdd76ff;">the market opens, and sleeps for at least eight hours a night—falls into this category. And it’s hard to argue with Warren’s success.</span>  
<span style="font-size:11.142857551574707pt;color:#ffdd76ff;">So which approach is right?</span> 
<span style="font-size:11.142857551574707pt;color:#ffdd76ff;">Here is a simple truth about productivity that not enough people write about:</span> <span style="font-size:11.142857551574707pt;color:#ffdd76ff;"><b>if you care enough about getting your work done, you’ll almost always find ways to do it.</b></span> <span style="font-size:11.142857551574707pt;color:#ffdd76ff;">Without thinking about it too much, you’ll align your time, attention, and energy towards greater productivity.</span> 
<span style="font-size:11.142857551574707pt;color:#ffdd76ff;">It’s for this reason that you may not need productivity advice on days when you have a rapidly-approaching deadline; or when someone is breathing down your neck to ship a big project. Or why, on days when you’re working late on a passion project, you probably don’t need to pick up a productivity book to figure out how to keep going. Productivity comes easily when you care about what you’re doing or have a strong incentive to get things done.</span>  
<span style="font-size:11.142857551574707pt;color:#ffdd76ff;">I personally fall into the second camp—my favorite way of becoming more productive is by working calmly and deliberately. This mindset also leads me to enjoy what I’m doing more. A few examples of how this plays out in practice:</span> 
- <span style="font-size:11.142857551574707pt;color:#ffdd76ff;">Instead of working on more things, faster, I step back so I can work on</span> <span style="font-size:11.142857551574707pt;color:#ffdd76ff;"><i>the right things</i></span> <span style="font-size:11.142857551574707pt;color:#ffdd76ff;">deliberately and with intention.</span>  
- <span style="font-size:11.142857551574707pt;color:#ffdd76ff;">Instead of working at a frenzied pace, I approach tasks with a calm deliberateness that allows me to accomplish more in the same amount of time—what I lose in speed I more than make up for in intentionality.</span> 
- <span style="font-size:11.142857551574707pt;color:#ffdd76ff;">Instead of cramming my day with commitments, I take time to think, reflect, and daydream—leaving plenty of white space in my schedule whenever I can.</span>  
- <span style="font-size:11.142857551574707pt;color:#ffdd76ff;">Instead of hustling and working insane hours, I try to develop systems that accomplish my work tasks</span> <span style="font-size:11.142857551574707pt;color:#ffdd76ff;"><i>for me</i></span><span style="font-size:11.142857551574707pt;color:#ffdd76ff;">, so I have time for more meaningful things (like reading and strategizing).</span>  
- <span style="font-size:11.142857551574707pt;color:#ffdd76ff;">Instead of taking on more than I can handle, I delegate.</span>  
<span style="font-size:11.142857551574707pt;color:#ffdd76ff;"><b>These two productivity mindsets lead us to accomplish our work in different ways.</b></span> <span style="font-size:11.142857551574707pt;color:#ffdd76ff;">Generally speaking, hustlers devote more</span> <span style="font-size:11.142857551574707pt;color:#ffdd76ff;"><i>time and energy</i></span> <span style="font-size:11.142857551574707pt;color:#ffdd76ff;">to their tasks, while those who work deliberately devote more</span> <span style="font-size:11.142857551574707pt;color:#ffdd76ff;"><i>thoughtfulness</i></span><span style="font-size:11.142857551574707pt;color:#ffdd76ff;">. Those who work calmly and deliberately are often also quite lazy, and it’s for this reason they look for shortcuts—ways of automating, delegating, and systematizing their work so they have even more time to relax and reflect. While it’s the immediate sense of accomplishment that comes from busyness that makes hustlers feel important, those who work calmly feel a sense of importance when their work makes a difference.</span>  
<span style="font-size:11.142857551574707pt;color:#ffdd76ff;">I genuinely believe that working calmly and deliberately gives me a slight edge—but at the same time, I’d be foolish to argue against those who achieve great conventional success by working faster and harder. That said, there could be one variable skewing these results: someone who cares about how much they accomplish at work may be</span> <span style="font-size:11.142857551574707pt;color:#ffdd76ff;"><i>more likely</i></span> <span style="font-size:11.142857551574707pt;color:#ffdd76ff;">to become a hustler—a mentality in which you center your life around the perceived importance of work. Assuming that one prioritizes accomplishment the same amount under both mindsets, though, working deliberately gives you an edge. If you have the privilege and ability to slow down and work with greater intention, I highly recommend it.</span> 
<span style="font-size:11.142857551574707pt;color:#ffdd76ff;">When it comes to our productivity, calm and deliberateness matter—especially right now.</span> <span style="font-size:11.142857551574707pt;color:#ffdd76ff;"><b>In today’s anxious world, the path to greater productivity runs straight through calm.</b></span> <span style="font-size:11.142857551574707pt;color:#ffdd76ff;">This is why I’ve been writing so much about the topic lately.</span>  
<span style="font-size:11.142857551574707pt;color:#ffdd76ff;">Hustling can be a sign you’re not working strategically enough.</span> <span style="font-size:11.142857551574707pt;color:#ffdd76ff;"><b>Instead, become more calm and deliberate.</b></span> <span style="font-size:11.142857551574707pt;color:#ffdd76ff;">You’ll get your work done—and you’ll have a lot more fun while you’re at it.</span>
