<span style="font-size:11.142857551574707pt;">Most people aren't consciously aware that they are putting off a task or project. The simplest solution is to create a habit where you track your impulses to procrastinate.  Four ideas to help to create this habit:</span>
- <span style="font-size:11.142857551574707pt;">Create Reminders.</span>  <span style="font-size:11.142857551574707pt;color:#333333ff;">Write notes to yourself on pieces of paper, and put them around the areas you usually procrastinate. Create a desktop picture/wallpaper with the words:</span> <span style="font-size:11.142857551574707pt;color:#333333ff;"><i>be aware!</i></span> <span style="font-size:11.142857551574707pt;color:#333333ff;">Have reminders pop up on your computer or phone to be aware.</span> 
- <span style="font-size:11.142857551574707pt;color:#333333ff;">Use Tally Marks. Anotando cada vez que se siente el deseo de postergar. Este sencillo acto funciona como una forma de construir consciencia de tu deseo de postergar/procrastinar.</span>  
- <span style="font-size:11.142857551574707pt;color:#333333ff;">Log It Daily. Anotando cada día cuando se ha sido consciente de este hábito. De forma similar a como se haría para cualquier otro hábito.</span> 
- <span style="font-size:11.142857551574707pt;color:#333333ff;">Use a Rubber Band. Cuando sientas la necesidad/ impulso (urge) de procrastinar  cambia la pulsera de una muñeca a otra.</span>  
- <span style="font-size:11.142857551574707pt;color:#333333ff;">Ask yourself a few important questions whenever you feel the urge to put of a task or project.</span>  
	- <span style="font-size:11.142857551574707pt;color:#333333ff;"><i>What reason do I have for putting off this task?</i></span> 
	- <span style="font-size:11.142857551574707pt;color:#333333ff;"><i>Why do  I feel it's so tough to do?</i></span> 
	- <span style="font-size:11.142857551574707pt;color:#333333ff;"><i>How many times have I successfully done it in the past?</i></span> 
	- <span style="font-size:11.142857551574707pt;color:#333333ff;"><i>What did I do then to get started?</i></span> 
	- <span style="font-size:11.142857551574707pt;color:#333333ff;"><i>What is the easiest step that I  can do right now to get started?</i></span>  

<span style="font-size:11.142857551574707pt;color:#333333ff;">When you develop the awareness habit, you will start to recognize the specific patterns and triggers that cause you to skip an activity. A partir de ese punto se puede generar un plan de actuación para cuando se detecte el impulso de procrastinar.</span>
